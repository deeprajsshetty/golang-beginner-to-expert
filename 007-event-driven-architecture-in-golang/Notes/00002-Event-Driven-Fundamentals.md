# Part 1: Event-Driven Fundamentals

This first part will provide an understanding of what event-driven architecture (EDA) is and the benefits of using it for your next application. We will also be introduced to the application we will be working with. This part will also cover some helpful patterns that can be useful for the adoption and development of EDA. Then, it will introduce methods for planning an application using EventStorming.

## Chapters in This Part

### Chapter 1: Introduction to Event-Driven Architecture

This chapter will introduce the fundamental concepts of Event-Driven Architecture (EDA). It will cover what EDA is, why it’s important, and how it can help you design scalable, resilient, and responsive systems. We will also look at the key components of an event-driven system, including events, event producers, event consumers, and event brokers.

### Chapter 2: Supporting Patterns in Brief

In this chapter, we will explore some of the common patterns that can support the adoption and development of event-driven systems. These patterns will help you address challenges such as event sourcing, message queues, event storage, and eventual consistency. The chapter will provide brief overviews of these patterns and their relevance in different scenarios.

### Chapter 3: Design and Planning

This chapter will introduce EventStorming, a collaborative method for designing and planning an event-driven application. EventStorming helps you identify the key events in your system, clarify business processes, and align your team’s understanding of the domain. We will explore the steps involved in EventStorming and how to use this method to create an actionable plan for your event-driven architecture.
